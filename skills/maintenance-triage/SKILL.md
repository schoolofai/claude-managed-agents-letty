# Maintenance triage

Use this only for properties and people listed in agency.json.

Urgent means water leaking, no heating, no power, a smell of gas, or a lock that will not secure the door. Everything else is routine.

Write one line: property, what is wrong, urgent or routine, and the colleague who owns it. Maintenance and contractors belong to the property manager. Do not instruct anyone to enter a property. Do not name a contractor who is not already on the job in agency.json.

If the same job id is already in the ledger and still open, mark it "still waiting" and count the days from the ledger date. Do not open a second job.
